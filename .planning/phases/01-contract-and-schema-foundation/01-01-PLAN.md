| Audit history -> future readers/delivery | Future timeline and Watch Duty consumers may expose audit payloads. |

| T-01-01-02 | Spoofing | `actor_id` contract | mitigate | Document one required namespaced `actor_id` and state admin actions use trusted human identity, not request-body actor fields or transport service accounts. |
| T-01-01-03 | Repudiation | deletion audit semantics | mitigate | Document `feed.deleted.before_values` as the self-contained deleted-feed snapshot and state audit history must not rely on the `feeds` row after hard delete. |
| T-01-01-04 | Information Disclosure | future audit payload exposure | mitigate | Document that Phase 1 adds no admin read API/UI and that future consumers derive payloads from the domain contract under their own authorization boundary. |
