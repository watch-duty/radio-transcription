<threat_model>
Trust boundaries:
- GCS `config.json` is the eval source of truth.
- Local `results/` can be stale, missing, or from another workstation.
- Summary artifacts are operator-facing evidence for metric decisions.

STRIDE register:
- DUR-T7 stale local summary: relying on local `results/` hides the durable
  report state. Mitigation: upload summary JSON/Markdown to stable GCS paths
  after successful eval.
- DUR-T8 partial-success spoofing: summary uploads before scoring completes can
  advertise a failed eval. Mitigation: upload summaries only after target
  predictions, normalized manifest upload, metrics, and report construction
  succeed.
- DUR-T9 target ambiguity: a loop over plural targets can produce more rows
  than the config contract allows. Mitigation: call `require_config_eval_model`
  and execute exactly one target.
</threat_model>
