<threat_model>
Trust boundaries:
- Balance reports summarize user-controlled manifests and may drive model
  selection decisions.

STRIDE register:
- T-02-07 Repudiation: a single score could hide poor bucket balance.
  Mitigation: report component deltas alongside the weighted score.
- T-02-08 Tampering: timestamps could be missing or malformed and skew scoring.
  Mitigation: timestamp/month/hour distributions are report-only and missing
  timestamps do not alter split assignment.
- T-02-09 Information disclosure: reports should not include raw rows.
  Mitigation: balance reports aggregate counts, durations, buckets, and group
  IDs, not raw manifest payloads.
</threat_model>
