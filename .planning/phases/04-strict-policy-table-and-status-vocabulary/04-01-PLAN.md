<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| collector/runtime evidence -> policy | Structured status and evidence determine whether a feed budget can be consumed. |
| policy -> runtime caller | Policy returns pure intent; runtime decides side effects later. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-01-01 | Tampering | `classify_failure_policy(...)` | mitigate | Replace broad owner defaults with explicit status/evidence rules and mismatch tests. |
| T-04-01-02 | Repudiation | policy decisions | mitigate | Assert stable `PolicyIntent`, `ExecutedAction`, and budget/quarantine booleans per route. |
| T-04-01-03 | Denial of Service | accidental mass feed quarantine | mitigate | Unknown or mismatched evidence fails closed to telemetry gap, not budget/quarantine. |
| T-04-01-04 | Elevation of Privilege | policy module side effects | mitigate | Keep policy pure and prohibit storage, Pub/Sub, telemetry, replay, breaker, and alert calls. |
</threat_model>
