<threat_model>
Trust boundaries:
- The TOML config and configured manifests/source maps are user-controlled
  dataset-version inputs.
- GCS objects may be missing, malformed, stale, or pointed at the wrong scheme.

STRIDE register:
- T-01-01 Spoofing/tampering: local, HTTPS, or S3 paths could bypass the
  GCS-only input contract. Mitigation: config validation rejects non-`gs://`
  manifest/source-map/config URIs.
- T-01-02 Tampering: malformed JSON/JSONL could be partially accepted and
  produce an incomplete split input. Mitigation: strict readers raise
  `GcsInputError` with URI and line/context rather than skipping bad rows.
- T-01-03 Information disclosure: raw config or manifest payloads could be
  logged. Mitigation: errors include URI, row/line index, and reason, not full
  raw rows.
</threat_model>
