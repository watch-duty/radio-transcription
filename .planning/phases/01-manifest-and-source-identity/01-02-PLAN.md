<threat_model>
Trust boundaries:
- Manifest row fields and audio URLs are user-controlled dataset inputs.
- Built-in Echo CSV is trusted repo metadata but may be incomplete for future
  streams.

STRIDE register:
- T-01-04 Tampering: row fields could claim an incorrect source group.
  Mitigation: fixed family-specific cascades require explicit IDs or
  unambiguous parsable source identity.
- T-01-05 Information disclosure: raw row payloads may contain labels or
  operational URLs. Mitigation: `raw_row` stays internal and diagnostics use
  limited fields.
- T-01-06 Repudiation/integrity: ambiguous Echo names could be guessed,
  causing train/eval source leakage. Mitigation: Echo requires both
  `area_code` and `echo_name`; ambiguous name-only rows fail.
</threat_model>
