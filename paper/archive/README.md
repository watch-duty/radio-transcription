# Internal legacy evidence archive

The subtree `legacy_reference_history_study/` preserves an earlier LaTeX paper
snapshot and its generated tables, figures, macros, and receipts for internal
provenance only. That study served labeled/reference transcripts as history and
is not deployment-admissible under the current paper's prediction-only policy.

Nothing in this archive is an authoritative paper source, a current result, or
a release candidate. The files are excluded from the current manuscript,
figures, tables, claims, and public artifact allowlist. They must not be moved
back to conventional names such as `paper/main.pdf` or regenerated into the
canonical paper directories.

The authoritative manuscript is [`../main.md`](../main.md), with the current
prediction-only appendix at [`../appendix.md`](../appendix.md).
